## 2. What happens next

**01 — Discovery and kickoff**  
We confirm your goals, audience, required pages, key user actions, stakeholders, dependencies, and success criteria.

**02 — Structure and creative direction**  
We establish the site structure and visual direction. You review the agreed materials at the approval points in your scope.

**03 — Design and content approval**  
We present the agreed designs and content for review. Your nominated decision-maker consolidates feedback so decisions remain clear.

**04 — Development and preview**  
We build the approved experience and provide a preview/staging version for review against the agreed scope.

**05 — Quality assurance and launch approval**  
We test the agreed pages and workflows, resolve launch-blocking defects, and request written approval for the specific version intended for production.

**06 — Launch and handover**  
After the contractual launch conditions are met, we deploy, complete post-launch checks, and transfer the agreed documentation and access.

The exact sequence, deliverables, revision allowance, payment milestones, and dates are governed by your accepted proposal and agreement. Dates can depend on timely content, access, feedback, third-party providers, and approvals.

## 3. What we need from you

Please nominate one primary contact and one person authorized to make final decisions. Depending on your project, we will also need:

- Your business goals, audiences, and priority user actions.
- Approved logo files, brand guidelines, photographs, and other licensed assets.
- Accurate service, team, organization, contact, and location information.
- Existing website/domain details and the names of the people who administer them.
- One consolidated set of feedback at each review stage.
- Timely approval of content, design, staging, and launch.
- A designated subject-matter reviewer for any regulated, clinical, legal, or otherwise specialist content.

Use the supplied Asset & Content Collection Checklist to track what is needed. Items that do not apply can be marked “Not applicable.”

## 6. Secure sharing and privacy

Please do **not** send passwords, API keys, patient records, medical histories, applicant CVs, or other sensitive personal information through ordinary email or this welcome document. We will agree an appropriate access method for accounts and an approved secure workflow for any sensitive data that is genuinely required. Do not assume a standard marketing form is suitable for health information.

Only share photographs, testimonials, fonts, logos, and other assets that your organization is authorized to use. Please identify any restrictions, expiry dates, attribution requirements, or permissions that apply.
