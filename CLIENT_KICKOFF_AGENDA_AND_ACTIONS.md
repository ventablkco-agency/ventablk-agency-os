## Part A — Kickoff agenda

**Recommended duration:** 45–60 minutes. Shorten or extend according to project complexity.

### 1. Welcome and introductions — 5 minutes
- Confirm each attendee’s role and decision authority.
- Identify the primary day-to-day contact, final approver, and backup contact.
- Confirm the purpose of the meeting and the decisions required.

### 2. Goals, audiences, and success — 8 minutes
- What business or user problem should the website solve?
- Who are the priority audiences and what do they need to do?
- What is the primary conversion/action?
- How will success be measured, and what baseline data exists?

### 3. Scope and page structure — 8 minutes
- Review the accepted scope and exclusions.
- Confirm required pages, key content, and functional features.
- Identify assumptions and dependencies that need written confirmation.
- Record new requests separately; do not treat them as automatically included.

### 4. Brand, design, and content — 8 minutes
- Review available brand guidelines and approved design references.
- Confirm content and asset owners, delivery dates, and permission requirements.
- Agree who verifies factual, clinical, legal, or specialist claims.
- Confirm how consolidated feedback and formal approvals will be submitted.

### 5. Technical access and integrations — 8 minutes
- Identify domain, DNS, hosting, repository, analytics, and business-profile owners where relevant.
- Confirm integrations, form destinations, notification recipients, and operational owners.
- Discuss access using delegated roles or the approved secure method.
- Flag privacy/security requirements and sensitive-data workflows for separate assessment before implementation.

### 6. Timeline, reviews, and communication — 8 minutes
- Walk through milestones, dependencies, review windows, and the target launch window.
- Confirm communication channel and agreed check-in cadence.
- Agree expected client response windows; do not assume unconfirmed service levels.
- Identify risks that could affect timing and how they will be escalated.

### 7. Recap and next steps — 5 minutes
- Read back decisions and unresolved questions.
- Assign an owner and due date to every action.
- Confirm the next milestone and next review date.
- Confirm when the written meeting summary will be shared.
