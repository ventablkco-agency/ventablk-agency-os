# Client Asset & Content Collection Checklist
**VentaBlk Co. — Website Project Intake**  
**Use:** Copy this checklist into the client’s approved project workspace and share it after onboarding. Request only items relevant to the agreed scope.

## How to use this checklist

For each item, record **Owner**, **Status**, **Due date**, and **Secure location / notes** in the client workspace. Suggested statuses: Not requested, Requested, Received, Needs clarification, Approved, Not applicable.

Do not place client-specific private files or sensitive information in the public agency OS repository. Do not request passwords by email or store them in this checklist.

## 1. Organization and contact details

- [ ] Approved public organization name and spelling.
- [ ] Short organization overview and approved history.
- [ ] Public address, map location, phone numbers, email, opening hours, and service area.
- [ ] Approved emergency or urgent-contact instructions, if applicable.
- [ ] Primary contact, final decision-maker, and backup contact.
- [ ] Design approver, content approver, and specialist reviewer(s).
- [ ] Required languages and who verifies translations.

## 2. Brand assets

- [ ] Primary logo — preferably vector SVG/PDF, plus transparent PNG.
- [ ] Alternate, monochrome, light/dark, or icon logo variants, if available.
- [ ] Brand guidelines or identity manual.
- [ ] Approved color values and typography/font files or references.
- [ ] Existing icons, illustrations, patterns, and brand graphics.
- [ ] Brand voice/tone examples and prohibited uses.
- [ ] Written confirmation of rights/licenses for supplied assets.
- [ ] Font, stock-photo, illustration, and other third-party license restrictions recorded.

## 3. Photography and media

- [ ] Approved hero/banner imagery.
- [ ] Facility, office, product, service, or location photographs.
- [ ] Team/doctor/staff headshots, where relevant.
- [ ] Approved video, animation, or audio assets, if in scope.
- [ ] Captions, names, roles, and descriptive alt-text context where needed.
- [ ] Written permission/model releases for identifiable people where required.
- [ ] Patient/customer images and testimonials reviewed for authorization and applicable rules.
- [ ] Image usage restrictions, attribution, expiry, and crop requirements recorded.
- [ ] Higher-resolution originals supplied where available; avoid screenshots of images where originals exist.

## 4. Copy and factual content

- [ ] Home page messaging and primary call to action.
- [ ] About/organization copy.
- [ ] Service/product/department descriptions.
- [ ] Team profiles, job titles, qualifications, and credentials (where relevant).
- [ ] Contact, directions, hours, accessibility, and visitor information.
- [ ] FAQs, policies, downloadable resources, and supporting materials.
- [ ] Approved calls to action and destination details.
- [ ] Privacy notice, cookie notice, terms, disclaimers, and consent language as applicable.
- [ ] Testimonials, reviews, awards, statistics, and case studies verified and authorized.
- [ ] Source/owner and final approver recorded for each content item.
- [ ] Old URLs/content to preserve, redirect, archive, or remove identified.

## 5. Healthcare or regulated content — where applicable

- [ ] Named authorized clinical/specialist reviewer.
- [ ] Services and department descriptions verified.
- [ ] Staff names, specialties, qualifications, and registration details checked by the client.
- [ ] Medical claims, outcomes, emergency guidance, and treatment descriptions reviewed by an authorized person.
- [ ] Photos, patient stories, testimonials, and consent/permission reviewed.
- [ ] Clear list of content that must not be published.
- [ ] Any health-data collection requirement flagged for separate privacy/security assessment before design or implementation.

Do not infer credentials, treatment outcomes, clinical claims, or emergency instructions. The client must verify specialist facts before publication.

## 6. Sitemap and page requirements

- [ ] Required pages and page priority confirmed.
- [ ] Navigation labels and page relationships confirmed.
- [ ] Required page sections and calls to action identified.
- [ ] Existing page URLs and important inbound links inventoried.
- [ ] Content owner and target delivery date assigned for every required page.
- [ ] Missing copy/images logged as dependencies rather than silently replaced with invented facts.

## 7. Functional and integration inputs

- [ ] Contact/enquiry form requirements.
- [ ] Appointment/booking requirements and provider contact.
- [ ] Careers/application workflow and authorized HR owner, if applicable.
- [ ] CMS/editing requirements and intended editors.
- [ ] Search, filtering, maps, downloads, or other agreed features.
- [ ] Analytics/conversion requirements and authorized account owner.
- [ ] Email/CRM/third-party integration contact and documentation.
- [ ] Form fields, submission recipients, storage, access, retention, and failure handling agreed.
- [ ] Privacy/consent requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Sensitive data requirements escalated before any standard form is selected.

## 8. Domain, hosting, and technical access

- [ ] Current website URL and known platform/stack.
- [ ] Domain registrar and named account owner.
- [ ] DNS provider and authorized administrator.
- [ ] Hosting/CDN provider and authorized account owner.
- [ ] Existing repository/source-code location and owner.
- [ ] Analytics and Search Console properties and account owner.
- [ ] Business listing/profile URL and authorized manager, if in scope.
- [ ] Existing forms, booking systems, CRM, email, or other integrations inventoried.
- [ ] Access method agreed: delegated access, role-based invitation, or approved password manager.
- [ ] Backup, rollback, and launch window constraints recorded.
- [ ] Third-party subscriptions, licenses, renewals, and billing owner identified.

**Never place passwords, API keys, recovery codes, private tokens, or other secrets here.** Record the account owner and approved access route only. Grant least-privilege access and remove temporary access when no longer needed.

## 9. Search and measurement inputs

- [ ] Existing sitemap and robots.txt location, if known.
- [ ] Existing redirects or URLs that must be retained.
- [ ] Search Console and analytics access owner.
- [ ] Current performance/search baseline, if available.
- [ ] Priority services/topics/locations confirmed.
- [ ] Business name, address, phone, and hours consistency checked.
- [ ] Measurement goals and reporting expectations agreed.

## 12. Intake completion gate

Before detailed design or production begins, confirm:

- [ ] Required assets are received or explicitly tracked as dependencies.
- [ ] Public facts and claims have an identified client-side verifier.
- [ ] Rights and permissions are confirmed for assets intended for publication.
- [ ] Missing content has an owner and target date.
- [ ] Domain/hosting/access ownership and access method are known.
- [ ] Functional requirements and data-handling needs are understood.
- [ ] Required approvers and review windows are documented.
- [ ] Scope and any change implications are agreed in writing.
