## Mandatory design rules

1. **Alternate themes by document:** Document 1 black, Document 2 light, Document 3 black, Document 4 light, and continue alternating. A multi-page document keeps one consistent theme across its pages.
2. **Black theme:** near-black background, strong white typography, restrained grey rules and supporting text.
3. **Light theme:** warm off-white background, near-black typography, subtle neutral dividers.
4. **Typography:** bold, high-impact editorial hierarchy. Use heavy headline weights, readable body text, clear spacing and restrained supporting labels.
5. **Agency identity:** place the approved Ventablk logo asset in the header or appropriate brand location. Keep proportions intact and use the right contrast variant.
6. **Client logo:** include a clean, clearly named client-logo placeholder wherever appropriate. Replace it only with client-approved artwork.
7. **Reusable fields:** use obvious placeholders for client name, business name, project name, reference number, dates, fees, payment milestones, deliverables, contact details and approval/signature fields.
8. **Commercial accuracy:** include only services agreed for that specific client. Do not imply that maintenance, hosting, third-party subscriptions, SEO, content, or additional revisions are included unless the signed scope says so.
9. **Confidentiality:** keep blank reusable masters in this public repository. Never add signed agreements, client-specific invoices, credentials, personal data, patient data or confidential client materials.
10. **Quality check:** inspect all pages at presentation size before delivery. Check clipping, overlaps, logo contrast, placeholder consistency, page numbering, spelling, price arithmetic and readability. Export final client documents only after review.

## When asked to create a client document

1. Open the document master library above first.
2. Find the closest approved master and duplicate it; do not redesign from scratch unless the user explicitly requests a new direction.
3. Place the actual approved VENTABLK logo asset from the source file.
4. Apply the correct alternating theme according to the document's position in the set.
5. Add the client logo placeholder and replace it only when approved client artwork is available.
6. Fill in only verified client details; leave unknown details as explicit placeholders.
7. Review the finished layout and share the Figma page/link plus a clear status of what is complete and what still needs client input.
