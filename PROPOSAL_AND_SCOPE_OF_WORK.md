## 8. Revision and change-control policy

- Included design revision rounds: [Number] at [defined stages].
- Included content corrections: [Scope and time window].
- A revision round means one consolidated list from the designated client approver.
- A defect is a failure to meet the accepted specification; a new feature, new page, or changed direction is a change request.
- For each change request, document the requested outcome, fee impact, timeline impact, and written approval before work begins.
- No change to scope or recurring fee takes effect without written agreement.

## 9. Client responsibilities

The client agrees to:
- Provide accurate business information, approved copy, assets, and permissions on time.
- Identify one decision-maker and provide consolidated feedback by agreed dates.
- Verify hospital services, clinician credentials, medical claims, opening hours, emergency instructions, and other factual/clinical content before publication.
- Provide authorized access to domain, hosting, analytics, and business listings through secure delegated access.
- Approve privacy notices, data handling, and third-party services where required.
- Pay invoices according to the agreed terms and notify the agency of changes to business information.

## 10. Ownership, licenses, and access

Define explicitly in the signed agreement:
- Ownership/license of final custom deliverables after payment.
- Ownership and license terms for pre-existing agency tools, templates, reusable components, and know-how.
- Third-party and open-source licenses.
- Domain, hosting, repository, analytics, and business profile ownership.
- Access to source code, design files, and documentation at handover.
- Use of project screenshots in the agency portfolio, requiring client permission where appropriate.
- Any licensed assets or subscriptions that must remain active.

Do not promise transfer of third-party assets beyond their licenses. Do not withhold client-owned domains or accounts as leverage in a dispute; follow the signed agreement and applicable law.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

The project is ready for client acceptance when the agreed scope is implemented, agreed QA checks are recorded, critical launch blockers are resolved, content has been approved by the client, and any known exceptions are listed. Acceptance should identify the tested staging/production version and be recorded in writing.
