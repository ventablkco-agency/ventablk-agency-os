# Website Discovery Questionnaire
**VentaBlk Co. — Client Brief**  
**Purpose:** Gather the business, audience, content, functional, and operational information needed to scope and plan a website project.  
**Completion guidance:** Answer what you can. Mark unknown items “Not sure” and items that do not apply “N/A.” We will confirm open questions together.

> **Privacy note:** Do not include passwords, API keys, patient/customer records, medical histories, applicant CVs, payment-card data, or other sensitive personal information. Use the approved secure workspace for files. For healthcare or other regulated workflows, discuss requirements with us before collecting or processing sensitive data.

## B. Business goals and success

1. Why are you commissioning or redesigning the website now?
2. What are the top three outcomes the website should support?
3. What is the single most important action a visitor should take? (For example: call, request an appointment, submit an enquiry, book a consultation, or learn about a service.)
4. What secondary actions matter?
5. Who are your primary audiences? Describe their needs in broad, non-sensitive terms.
6. Which locations, markets, or languages should the site serve?
7. What should visitors understand or trust after using the site?
8. How will you judge whether the website is successful?
9. Are there business constraints, deadlines, campaigns, or events we should know about?

## C. Current website and context

1. Current website URL, if any:
2. What works well on the current site?
3. What is not working or must change?
4. Which pages or URLs must be retained?
5. Are there known technical, accessibility, performance, search, or content problems?
6. Who currently manages the website, domain, DNS, hosting, analytics, and business listings?
7. Are there existing analytics or search reports you are authorized to share?
8. Are there competitor or reference websites? For each, what specifically do you like or dislike?

## D. Brand and creative direction

1. Do you have brand guidelines, a logo, approved colors, and fonts?
2. Which words describe the desired experience? (For example: confident, warm, editorial, minimal, clinical, playful, premium.)
3. Which styles or approaches should we avoid?
4. Do you prefer existing brand continuity or a broader refresh?
5. What approved photography, illustrations, video, or other assets are available?
6. Are there accessibility, language, cultural, or device considerations?
7. Who has final design approval?
