## 4. Design revisions and changes

The project fee includes **two consolidated design revision rounds** at the agreed design review stages. The client should combine feedback from its decision-makers into one clear list per round.

A revision round adjusts the agreed design direction. A new page, new feature, new integration, new content request outside the agreed scope, or a substantial change after approval is additional work.

Before extra work starts, Ventablk will describe the change, any additional fee, and any timeline impact. The client must approve these in writing. Silence is not approval.

## 5. Client responsibilities and approvals

The client will:

- Appoint one primary contact authorised to consolidate feedback and approve deliverables.
- Review drafts and provide feedback or approval within [agreed number] business days.
- Verify factual claims, service details, prices, contact information and, for healthcare clients, all clinical information, clinician credentials, emergency instructions and medical claims before publication.
- Obtain any permissions, licences, internal approvals and legal/privacy notices required for the supplied material and the client's business.
- Provide Ventablk with authorised access needed to perform the agreed work, using delegated access where the provider supports it.

Missing content, delayed feedback, inaccessible accounts or delayed third-party services may change the timeline. Ventablk will communicate material delays and agree revised dates with the client.

Ventablk may set them up, administer them, publish updates or maintain them on the client's behalf when the client has authorised that access. Ventablk does not become the owner merely because it performs the administration.

The client remains responsible for provider charges and renewals unless the accepted proposal expressly assigns a specific payment task to Ventablk. If Ventablk pays an agreed provider charge on the client's behalf, the reimbursement amount and timing must be stated in writing.

Access should use provider-supported delegated roles wherever possible. Passwords and secret keys must not be sent in ordinary documents or committed to a repository. The client must not grant access beyond what is reasonably required for the agreed work.

## 9. Review, acceptance and launch

Ventablk will provide the agreed review version or staging link. The client will test the agreed pages and functions and provide one consolidated list of issues by [review deadline].

Before launch, the client must approve the final content and confirm that the agreed scope is ready to publish. Launch is subject to receipt of the payment due before launch and availability of required third-party accounts.

Requests that fall outside the agreed specification will be handled as change requests. Known issues or agreed exceptions should be documented before acceptance.

## 10. Post-launch defect-fix period

Ventablk provides a **14-calendar-day defect-fix period from the production launch date**.

During this period, Ventablk will address reproducible defects where the delivered work fails to match the agreed written specification, at no additional project fee.

This period does not include:
- New features, new pages, design changes or additional content.
- Issues caused by client edits, third-party providers, unsupported software changes, expired subscriptions, or changes made by another provider.
- Ongoing updates, monitoring, backups, security administration or general support unless these are part of a separate maintenance agreement.

The client should report a suspected defect with its page, steps to reproduce it and supporting details. Ventablk will assess whether it falls within this period and agree a reasonable correction plan. This period is not a promise of zero defects or uninterrupted service.

## 12. Third-party services and outcomes

Some website features depend on hosting providers, domain registrars, analytics platforms, search engines, map listings, plugins, APIs or other third parties. Those services may change their features, policies, prices or availability.

Ventablk will perform the third-party setup tasks listed in the accepted scope but cannot guarantee search rankings, AI answer inclusion, a specific number of leads, uninterrupted third-party service or business outcomes that are not under its control.
